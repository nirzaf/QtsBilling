### Architectural rules encoded in the schema

- **Tenant integrity:** every business-owned row carries `business_id`; composite `(id, business_id)`
  alternate keys plus composite foreign keys make cross-business linkage structurally impossible on the
  stock/money join paths (F-22).
- **Immutable ledger:** posted journal entries are validated as balanced at **commit** by deferred
  constraint triggers; header totals must equal line sums (F-03/F-04).
- **Movement-derived stock:** balances are equal to the sum of their movements at every commit, and every
  movement must update its balance row in the same transaction; `on_hand >= 0` is a table check (F-01/F-02/F-12).
- **Append-only tables:** `inventory_movements`, `journal_lines`, `sale_lines`, `invoice_lines`,
  `purchase_order_lines`, `cash_register_movements`, `audit_events`, `processed_operations`,
  `sync_changes`, `legacy_identifier_map` reject UPDATE/DELETE at the database (F-15).
- **Idempotency:** unique `(business_id, device_id, operation_id)` on `processed_operations` and unique
  `(business_id, reference_type, reference_id, movement_type)` on `inventory_movements`.
- **Exactness:** historical snapshot columns on all transaction lines (name/SKU/price/tax/discount);
  no stock or price lives on `products`.
- **No card data:** `payments` has PSP reference/brand/last-4 only — PAN and CVV columns cannot exist (F-07).
- **No mutable balances:** `accounts` has no balance column; balances derive from posted journal lines (F-11).

## Live verification (PostgreSQL 18.6 container `qtsbilling-pg`, port 5433)

```text
dotnet ef database update → Applying migration '20261019082345_InitialCreate' … 'AddIntegrityGuards' … Done
```

Guard behaviour proven with real SQL transactions:

| # | Scenario | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `UPDATE journal_entries SET status='Posted'` on an entry with debits 100 / credits 90 | **Rejected at COMMIT**: `Journal entry … is unbalanced at commit: debits 100.0000 <> credits 90.0000` |
| 2 | `UPDATE journal_lines …` on a posted entry | **Rejected**: `Table journal_lines is append-only` |
| 3 | `DELETE FROM journal_entries …` (cascades to lines) | **Rejected**: append-only guard on `journal_lines` |
| 4 | Movement inserted without updating its balance row | **Rejected at COMMIT**: `… leaves balance 0.000000 against movement total 5.000000` |
| 5 | Balance row updated to 9 while movements total 5 | **Rejected at COMMIT**: `… is 9.000000, but movements total 5.000000` |
| 6 | Movement + matching balance update in one transaction | **Accepted** (balance 5.000000, one movement) |
| 7 | `on_hand = -1` | **Rejected**: `ck_inventory_balances_on_hand_non_negative` |
| 8 | Balance referencing a location of business A and a variant of business B | **Rejected**: composite FK `(variant_id, business_id)` violation |
| 9 | Same customer phone in another business / duplicate in the same business | **Accepted / rejected** (partial unique index) |
| 10 | Draft invoice line edited before issuance / after `Issued` | **Accepted / rejected** (`Invoice lines are immutable once the invoice leaves draft`) |
