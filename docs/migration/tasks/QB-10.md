# QB-10 — Cash register sessions, truthful payments and receipts

**Status: DONE (baseline live verification; partial-refund extension verified by focused PostgreSQL integration tests).** Cash capture is drawer-tracked end to end: at most one open session per register, audited movements with reasons, expected cash derived from those movements, and a close that stores counted vs expected and the variance. Card payments can no longer report success — the method is disabled until a verified PSP integration exists (F-09). Receipts render from immutable sale snapshots. Cash refunds can return selected quantities over multiple requests, with explicit restock or damaged-write-off disposition, while preserving drawer and ledger accounting (spec §3.6).

## Deliverables

| Area | Contents |
|---|---|
| Registers | `GET /api/v1/pos/registers` (with the current session + live expected cash), `POST /api/v1/pos/registers` (permanent register of an active location). |
| Sessions | `POST /{id}/sessions` (open with an opening float; one open session per register via unique partial index `ux_cash_register_sessions_open`), `GET /{id}/sessions/current`, `GET /{id}/sessions` (history), `GET /{id}/sessions/{sessionId}`. |
| Movements | `POST /{id}/sessions/{sessionId}/movements` — `cashIn` (paid in) and `cashOut` (paid out) only; a positive amount and a mandatory reason are required (audited with actor and time). Sales and refunds record their own movement kinds. |
| Drawer arithmetic | Expected cash is always **derived** from the append-only movements: opening float + cash sale payments + paid-in − paid-out − refunds. Provisional (offline) cash is reported separately and never counts as confirmed money (QB-20 will populate it). |
| Close | `POST /{id}/sessions/{sessionId}/close` with the counted amount: stores counted, expected, variance, closer and note, records a `closing` movement and returns the full reconciliation (breakdown + movements). |
| Payments | `card` is refused with `409 PAYMENT_METHOD_DISABLED` (no verified PSP — OPEN-6 default); cash, cheque and bank transfer remain (external methods keep the processing-reference requirement). A sale may name a `registerId`: the register must be active, at the sale location and have an open session (`409 CASH_REGISTER_NOT_OPEN` before any write; re-verified under `FOR UPDATE` inside the posting transaction). Cash taken on a register records a `salePayment` drawer movement. Registerless cash sales stay possible and are simply not part of any drawer reconciliation. |
| Receipts | `GET /api/v1/pos/sales/{id}/receipt` (JSON) and `?format=html` (printable, self-contained page, every value HTML-encoded). Content comes from the sale snapshots: a later product rename or price change never alters a receipt. Posted receipts carry a **FINAL RECEIPT — POSTED** banner; the template renders a visually distinct **PROVISIONAL / PENDING SYNC — NOT A FINAL RECEIPT** banner for offline captures (QB-19). |
| Refunds | `POST /api/v1/pos/sales/{id}/refund` (permission `sales.refund`, `Idempotency-Key` required): partial or full refunds of posted cash sales. Omitted/empty lines refund all remaining quantities; explicit lines are bounded by cumulative quantity already refunded. Each line chooses `Restock` or `DamagedWriteOff`; restocked goods create stock reversal movements, while damaged goods post shrinkage/COGS reversal without increasing on-hand stock. Each request posts its incremental balanced sales-return/tax/cash entry, creates an `R-<year>-#####` refund and line snapshots, records a drawer `refund` movement and audit event. The sale/payment reach reversed/refunded only after every sold quantity is refunded. `GET /{id}/refund` returns the latest refund and `GET /{id}/refunds` lists refund history. Card and transfer refunds remain unsupported. |
| Permissions | New `sales.refund` and `registers.manage`. Owner holds all; Manager holds both; Cashier holds `registers.manage` (opens/closes their own drawer) but not `sales.refund`. |
| Secret safety | PAN/CVV are structurally absent from contracts, entities and endpoints (only doc comments name them); the live secret scan reports 0 field declarations. |

## Decisions recorded

  sessions; provisional money is reported separately and never counted as expected.
