# QB-36 — The authority of an actor comes from the current membership, not from the session claims

**Status: DONE (the critical finding F07 is closed and proven against the real PostgreSQL).**

This is the critical part of task 04 of the *SMB Completion and Production Hardening* specification.

## What changed

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The permission handler read the **claims that were issued at sign-in**. A membership that was revoked, a user who was deactivated or an account that was locked out kept its full authority until the session cookie expired (eight hours). | The handler loads the **current** actor: the user must exist and be active, must not be locked out, and must hold an **active membership in the business the request claims to act for**. The permission is recomputed from the role that membership holds *now*. The claims only identify the actor; they never grant anything. |

The handler is registered as a **scoped** service, because it now reads the database through the request's
context (a singleton `DbContext` would be wrong).

## Evidence

`TestQtsBilling/Integration/BusinessAuthorizationTests.cs` — the real host, the real PostgreSQL:

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| T04.01 — a revoked membership | the cashier may post a sale while the membership is active; the membership is then revoked **while the session stays alive** and the next request is refused with `403` — the authority is gone immediately, not after the cookie expires. |
| T04.02 — a membership without the permission | a viewer is refused with `403` on a sale. |
| T04.03 — an anonymous caller | refused with `401`. |
| T04.04 — the owner | keeps the authority. |

## Decisions recorded

- **D-65** — the current authority (task 04): a permission is granted by the membership the actor holds
  at the moment of the request. A revoked membership, a deactivated user and a locked-out account lose
  their authority immediately; the claims of a session identify the actor and the business but never
  grant a permission.

## Still open in this slice

- The **authorization version** and the **session invalidation** (the sign-out of every affected
  session when a role changes) are not implemented: the authority is correct per request, but an open
  session is not actively terminated.
- The **location scope** (which locations an actor may act in) and the **granular permissions** of the
  specification (D02) are not implemented; the five base roles still carry their fixed permission sets.
- The **explicit business selection** (an actor with several memberships chooses one) is not
  implemented: the first active membership is used.
