# QB-38 — The stock carries a value and the cost of goods sold is the value (F15)

**Status: DONE (the critical finding F15 is closed and proven against the real PostgreSQL).**

This is task 15 of the *SMB Completion and Production Hardening* specification.

## What changed

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The cost of goods sold was `catalog purchase price × quantity`. The catalog price is not a costing engine: it is the *current* price of a product, not the value of the stock that was actually bought. A receipt moved the quantity and left the value nowhere. | Every stock movement carries an **authoritative value** and every balance carries the **value on hand** and the **moving average cost**. A receipt adds the acquisition value; an issue consumes the average; the cost of goods sold is the value the movement consumed. |

The schema (the migration `20261020083815_AddInventoryValuation`):

| Column | Table | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `value_on_hand` (18,4) | `inventory_balances` | the authoritative value of the stock, derived from the movements exactly like the quantity |
| `average_unit_cost` (18,8) | `inventory_balances` | the moving weighted average — informative, not the authority |
| `value_delta` (18,4) | `inventory_movements` | the value the movement added (positive) or consumed (negative) |

The rules of the calculation (specification D06):

- a **receipt** adds `quantity × the approved net acquisition value`;
- an **issue** consumes the average, and the **final depletion takes the exact remaining value**, so a
  quantity of zero implies a value of zero — the rounding never leaves a ghost value behind;
- the balance keeps `quantity = sum(movements)` **and** `value = sum(value deltas)`;
- **stock without a value cannot be consumed**: the issue is refused with `VALUATION_REQUIRED` instead
  of being costed at zero.

The stock that already existed carries no value after the migration — the historical valuation is a
finance decision (the blocker B02 of the readiness ledger), and until it is opened, consuming that
stock is refused rather than guessed.

## Evidence

`TestQtsBilling/Integration/InventoryValuationTests.cs` — the real host, the real PostgreSQL:

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| T15.01 — a receipt of 10 pieces at 60 | the value on hand is **600** and the average cost **60**; the movement carries the value delta 600. |
| T15.02 — a sale of 4 pieces with a **catalog price of 250** | the cost of goods sold is **240** (4 × the average cost of 60), the balance carries 6 pieces and the value 360, and every entry balances. The catalog price is not the cost. |
| T15.03 — the sale that empties the stock | the quantity is 0 **and** the value is 0 — the final depletion took the exact remaining value. |
| T15.04 — stock that was never valued | the sale is **refused** (`409`) and the stock is unchanged: the cost is never invented as zero. |

## Decisions recorded

- **D-67** — the perpetual valuation (task 15): the value of a stock movement is the authority. A
  receipt adds the acquisition value, an issue consumes the moving average, the final depletion takes
  the exact remaining value, and the cost of goods sold is the value the movement consumed — never the
  catalog price. Stock that carries no value cannot be consumed: the issue is refused with
  `VALUATION_REQUIRED` until a finance-approved opening valuation exists.
