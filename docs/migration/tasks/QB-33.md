# QB-33 — Payment truth: a customer credit is not collected money, and a cheque is not bank money

**Status: DONE (the defect is closed and the three cases run against the real PostgreSQL).**

This is the critical part of task 13 of the *SMB Completion and Production Hardening* specification. It
closes the first half of the critical finding **F14**: the sale posting used to record *every* tender
as a **confirmed** payment, so a customer-credit sale reported money that was never taken and a cheque
reported money that was never in the bank.

## What changed

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| A customer-credit sale created a **confirmed** payment of the full amount. | A customer-credit sale creates a **pending** tender and a **receivable**: the sale is unsettled, no confirmed payment exists, and the journal debits the receivables account. |
| A cheque or a bank transfer became a **confirmed** payment as soon as the client supplied a reference string, and the journal credited the **bank** account. | Only **cash** is settled by its receipt. A cheque or a transfer stays **pending** until an operator confirms the processing; the reference is the evidence for that review, not the proof. The amount stays on the receivables account until the collection is recorded. |

The sale carries the same truth: `PaymentStatus` is `Confirmed` only for a cash sale, `Pending`
otherwise. A cash refund still requires a confirmed payment, so a financed or unconfirmed sale can
never be paid out in cash.

## Evidence

`TestQtsBilling/Integration/PaymentTruthTests.cs` — the real host, the real PostgreSQL 18, a fresh
context for every assertion:

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| T13.01 — a cash sale | the sale and the payment are `Confirmed`, the entry balances, the header equals the sums of the lines. |
| T13.02 — a customer-credit sale | the sale is `Pending`, **no confirmed payment exists**, and the entry of *this sale* debits the receivables account with 115. |
| T13.03 — a cheque | the sale and the payment are `Pending` and the bank account of the business carries **0**: a reference string is not a proof that the money arrived. |

Every case also asserts that each journal entry balances on its own
(`FinancialAssertions.AssertEveryEntryBalancesAsync`).

## Decisions recorded

- **D-62** — the payment truth (task 13): only an acknowledged cash receipt settles a tender. A
  customer credit creates a receivable open item and never a confirmed payment; a cheque or a transfer
  stays unsettled until an operator confirms the processing, and it is not bank money in the meantime.
  The sale, the payment row and the journal agree on which state the money is in.
